The lens aggregates live-verified street targets while framing them with their measured accuracy — the numbers below are why its reports lead with humility instead of precision.

- **Bradshaw, M. T., Brown, L. D., & Huang, K. (2013).** Do Sell-Side Analysts Exhibit Differential Target Price Forecasting Ability? *Review of Accounting Studies, 18(4), 930–955.* <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-012-9216-5>
  For US 12-month targets (2000–2009): only 38% were met at the end of the horizon (64% touched at some point), absolute forecast errors averaged 45%, and target-implied returns exceeded realized returns by ~15% on average. The headline accuracy framing on every consensus report — targets are treated as opinions, not predictions.
- **Bilinski, P., Lyssimachou, D., & Walker, M. (2013).** Target Price Accuracy: International Evidence. *The Accounting Review, 88(3), 825–851.* <https://doi.org/10.2308/accr-50378>
  Across ~586,000 targets in 16 countries (2002–2009), prices touched the target during the horizon in 59% of cases (US ~55%), with mean absolute errors of ~45% (US ~50%). International corroboration that target inaccuracy is structural, not a bad-decade artifact.
- **Asquith, P., Mikhail, M. B., & Au, A. S. (2005).** Information Content of Equity Analyst Reports. *Journal of Financial Economics, 75(2), 245–282.* <https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=332662>
  Even star (Institutional Investor All-American) analysts' targets were achieved within the year only ~54% of the time (1997–1999). Why verified coverage breadth matters more to the lens than any single desk's conviction.
- **Brav, A., & Lehavy, R. (2003).** An Empirical Analysis of Analysts' Target Prices: Short-Term Informativeness and Long-Term Dynamics. *The Journal of Finance, 58(5), 1933–1968.* <https://doi.org/10.1111/1540-6261.00593>
  Target-price revisions move stock prices even after controlling for concurrent recommendation and earnings-forecast revisions; one-year targets averaged ~28% above the prevailing market price. Why the lens tracks target revisions and freshness (they carry information) while discounting the level (systematic optimism).
