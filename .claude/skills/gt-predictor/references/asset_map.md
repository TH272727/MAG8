## The "Least Priced-In" Screener

When running the Asymmetry Score, always check these metrics:
1. **% off all-time high**: The further an asset is from ATH with valid thesis → higher asymmetry
2. **Institutional accumulation vs. retail awareness gap**: Smart money in, retail not yet → early entry signal
3. **Implied volatility vs. historical volatility**: Low IV + high thesis conviction → buy calls
4. **Consensus rating gap**: If all major banks rate it "hold" or below while GT rates it "Tier 1" → maximum mispricing
5. **Coverage gap**: Assets with fewer than 3 major analyst price targets + strong GT thesis = least discovered

**How to build the current screen (run live each time):**
For each candidate the thesis points to, pull today's data and check:
1. **% off all-time high** — the further from ATH *with a valid, still-current thesis*, the higher the potential asymmetry (verify the discount is real now, not remembered).
2. **Institutional accumulation vs. retail awareness** — smart money in, retail not yet (13F/COT/ETF-flow data).
3. **Implied vs. historical volatility** — low IV + high thesis conviction favors optionality.
4. **Consensus rating gap** — banks rate it "hold"/below while the GT read is high-conviction.
5. **Coverage gap** — few analyst targets + strong thesis = least-discovered.

Do NOT reuse a stored list of names. Any specific "high-asymmetry" list is a point-in-time artifact; regenerate it from current prices, current positioning, and the *current* status of the thesis every time.

## Options Strategy by GT Phase

| Scenario | Structure | Strike | Expiry | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3–6mo before predicted event | Long OTM calls | 110–120% of current price | Expiry past catalyst | Maximum asymmetry; IV still low |
| 1–2mo before event | ATM call spread | Current to 115% | Expiry at/past event | Reduce premium, define risk |
| Event occurring | Long underlying | — | — | IV spike makes options expensive |
| Post-event consolidation | Covered calls | ATM | 30–45 DTE | Sell elevated IV, collect premium |
| Bear hedge (protect long book) | Put spread on index | 90–95% of index | 60–90 DTE | Cheap insurance when VIX low |

**IV positioning rule**: When GVZ (gold volatility) or VIX are below their 12-month average → buy premium (calls/puts). When above 12-month average → sell premium (spreads).
