# Event Templates: Pre-Built GT Analysis Frameworks

> **Live-data rule.** Any "current rung," dated position, price, or named live conflict below is an illustrative snapshot from when this file was written and is very likely stale. Before running a template, re-fetch the current state of the situation from news and primary sources and place *that* on the ladder — never reuse a stored position. The reusable content here is the *structure* (player questions, ladders, patterns), not the specific readings.

For each event type, this file provides the standard player map structure, key questions, law priorities, and asset lookup shortcuts. Load this when the event type is clear and you want to run a targeted, efficient analysis.

## Template 1: War / Military Conflict

**Law priority**: Asymmetry (1st), Escalation (2nd), Eschatological Convergence (3rd), Proximity (4th)

**Standard player map questions:**
- Who has escalation dominance on paper (weapons, budget)?
- Who has asymmetric advantages (motivation, geography, guerrilla capability)?
- What is each actor's narrative script — are they script-locked into escalation?
- Where are they on the escalation ladder? What triggers the next rung?
- Who are the spectators whose approval each side is courting?
- What is the Game Masters layer's interest — do they want escalation or containment?
- What is the exit condition for each actor — what does "winning" look like to their domestic audience?

**Key variables to track:**
- THAAD/interceptor stockpile depletion rates (asymmetric cost imposer)
- Strait/chokepoint status (Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Taiwan Strait)
- GCC/regional players' positioning (who's collecting the toll?)
- Oil price trajectory (war's economic translation)
- Fed response to inflation from oil shock

**Prediction structure:**
- Phase 1 (1–3 months): Initial kinetic phase — who gains what and at what cost?
- Phase 2 (3–6 months): Attrition and ceasefire pressure — who can sustain?
- Phase 3 (6–12 months): Structural resolution — does the empire declare victory and retreat, or escalate to next rung?
- Phase 4 (12–24 months): New equilibrium — who controls what resources?

**Asset shortcut**: See asset_map.md Category 1 (Wars and Military Conflicts)

**The "declare victory and go home" pattern**: When an empire faces the Law of Asymmetry in a protracted conflict, the most likely outcome is declaring victory on a technicality and withdrawing. Price this scenario for:
- Oil mean reversion (short)
- Gold hold/continue rise (de-dollarization structural, not war-dependent)
- Defense pause (short-term) then resume on next theater

## Template 2: Central Bank Policy Shift

**Law priority**: Elite Overproduction (1st), Game Masters (2nd), Eschatological Convergence (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What is the Fed's actual mandate vs. stated mandate? (Price stability vs. preventing Treasury default)
- What does the political principal (Treasury/White House) want the Fed to do?
- What does the Game Masters layer (bond market, primary dealers) want?
- Where is CPI vs. PCE vs. actual consumer inflation experience?
- What is the real yield trajectory (critical gold driver)?
- What is the Fed Chair's narrative script — inflation hawk or growth dove?
- What's the Treasury's financing need — what rate can the government actually afford?

**The "Fed Trap" diagnosis** (key test):
If YES to all three → Fed is fully trapped:
1. Inflation above 2.5% for 6+ consecutive months
2. GDP growth below 2.0% or contracting
3. Government cannot service debt at current rates without Fed intervention

**Prediction structure:**
- If trapped: Hold or cut (stagflation) → gold, hard assets, domestic stocks
- If cuts: Debasement acceleration → gold surge, miner leverage
- If surprise hike: Short-term gold correction, then buy the dip (structural thesis intact)
- "Yield curve control" scenario (ultimate trap): Explicit ceiling on long yields → hyperinflationary signal → maximum hard asset positioning

**Asset shortcut**: See asset_map.md Category 2 (Central Bank Trapped)

**Leading indicator calendar:**
- FOMC meeting dates: hold for rate decision + dot plot
- CPI release (monthly): inflation trajectory signal
- 30-year Treasury auction: bid-to-cover ratio signals foreign demand (declining = de-dollarization confirming)
- Fed balance sheet weekly: expanding = QE returning = debasement acceleration

## Template 3: Trade War Escalation

**Law priority**: Escalation (1st), Proximity (2nd), Game Masters (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What rung are we on the tariff escalation ladder?
- What is each side's domestic political narrative — what does their script demand?
- What are the actual economic leverage points (rare earths, food, semiconductors, energy)?
- Who can tolerate economic pain longer (higher pain threshold = higher M×E×C)?
- What is the Game Masters layer doing — are investment banks lobbying for de-escalation?
- Are there third-party beneficiaries of the trade war (Vietnam, India, Mexico) creating alternative equilibria?

**Escalation ladder for trade wars:**
Rung 1: Tariff threats and negotiations
Rung 2: Targeted tariffs (steel, aluminum, specific goods)
Rung 3: Broad reciprocal tariffs
Rung 4: Export controls (semiconductors, critical minerals)
Rung 5: Sanctions (financial system exclusion)
Rung 6: Seizure of assets (reserve freeze — Russia 2022 model)
Rung 7: Full economic decoupling

**Assessing the current rung**: Do not assume a rung. Pull the latest tariff actions, export-control announcements, and retaliation measures, then locate the conflict on the ladder from current evidence. Rungs can move in either direction within weeks.

**Asset shortcut**: See asset_map.md Category 7 (Trade War Escalation)

**The "truce with expiration" pattern**: Trade war truces are almost always temporary. The structural decoupling trend continues regardless of any single deal. Position around the truce announcement (buy the underlying, sell the escalation hedges temporarily), then re-enter escalation positioning 60–90 days later as the truce frays.

## Template 4: Regime Change / Political Transition

**Law priority**: Elite Overproduction (1st), Asymmetry (2nd), Eschatological Convergence (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What stage of the Turchin cycle is the target country in?
- What is the military's stance — aligned with the incumbent or restless?
- Who controls the key resources (oil, food, water, minerals) — do they support the incumbent?
- What is the external power's interest (US, China, Russia, regional hegemony)?
- Is there a credible alternative leader with a competing narrative script?
- What is the Game Masters layer's preferred outcome (resource access, strategic alignment)?

**Regime change patterns:**
- **Coup from within**: Military elite triggers when incumbent loses their confidence → sudden, low-violence transition
- **External facilitation**: Foreign power supports opposition → usually messier, longer
- **Popular uprising**: Elite overproduction Stage 4 reaches the street → most volatile, highest asset disruption
- **Managed transition**: Game Masters engineer soft landing → lowest volatility, best controlled outcome

**Asset approach:**
- Pre-event: Buy gold (capital flight hedge), short country ETF and sovereign bonds
- Event occurring: Maximum volatility — hold gold, avoid country-specific
- Post-event: If US-aligned new regime → country ETF long; if resource nationalist → commodity producer long

## Template 5: Currency Crisis

**Law priority**: Elite Overproduction (1st), Game Masters (2nd), Escalation (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What are FX reserves at and what's the burn rate?
- What is the external debt load in foreign currency?
- Is the IMF engaged — are capital controls imminent?
- What is the political will for austerity (the script determines whether they submit to IMF or default)?
- Is contagion risk real — are neighboring EM currencies correlated?

**Currency crisis stages:**
Stage 1: Speculative attack begins (reserve draw starts)
Stage 2: Capital controls imposed (convertibility suspended)
Stage 3: IMF bailout or default (binary)
Stage 4: Resolution — restructuring or hyperinflation

**Asset approach:**
- Early signal: Short country currency, long gold, short country banks
- Active crisis: Maximum gold, USD assets, Swiss franc
- Post-crisis (if IMF bailout): Recovery trade in restructured bonds (high yield, high risk)
- Post-crisis (if default): Short contagion EM, long USD

## Template 6: Resource Supply Disruption

**Law priority**: Proximity (1st), Escalation (2nd), Game Masters (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What percentage of global supply does this disruption affect?
- Are there alternative routes/suppliers that can absorb the disruption?
- What is the timeline for disruption to resolve (tactical vs. structural)?
- Who is the proximate loser (manufacturing dependent on this resource)?
- Who is the proximate winner (alternative supplier, substitute resource)?
- Is this disruption geopolitically engineered or accidental?

**The "structural vs. tactical" test:**
- Tactical disruption (weeks–months): Price spike → mean reversion. Trade the spike.
- Structural disruption (months–years): New supply chains, new suppliers, permanent price shift. Build a long position.

**Structural disruption signals:**
- Multiple actors with conflicting interests make resolution unlikely
- No alternative supply source exists within 12 months
- The disrupted region has been sanctioned (reducing re-supply options)
- Insurance and shipping costs have permanently re-rated

## Template 7: Alliance Formation / Dissolution

**Law priority**: Coordination (Universal Law) (1st), Eschatological Convergence (2nd), Game Masters (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What does this alliance enable that was impossible before?
- What is the narrative glue holding the alliance together (shared enemy vs. shared values vs. economic incentive)?
- Is the coordination strong (shared command, shared doctrine) or weak (political statement only)?
- What does the opposing alliance do in response?
- What Game Masters are shaping alliance membership and terms?

**Alliance formation signals:**
- Unusual summit compositions (unexpected attendees)
- Military interoperability exercises announced
- Currency/trade settlement changes (SWIFT alternatives, local currency deals)
- Financial infrastructure announcements (new development banks, payment rails)

**Asset implications:**
- New NATO/Western alliance expansion: Defense spending + domestic manufacturers in new members
- New BRICS+ expansion: Commodity diversification away from Western dollar system
- Alliance dissolution: Re-pricing of whatever the alliance underwrote (dollar system → gold; NATO → European defense ETFs)

## Template 8: Election / Political Transition (Democracy)

**Law priority**: Eschatological Convergence (1st), Elite Overproduction (2nd), Game Masters (3rd)

**Standard player map questions:**
- What narrative script does each candidate represent — who does their base NEED them to be?
- What does the outcome mean for fiscal policy (deficit expansion vs. austerity)?
- What does the outcome mean for monetary policy (Fed independence at risk)?
- What does the outcome mean for trade policy (tariffs, sanctions, deals)?
- What does the Game Masters layer want — are institutional incumbents backing one side?
- What is the Turchin cycle stage — is the electorate in Stage 3–4 restlessness?

**The "policy market" asset map:**
- Republican win (2026 archetype): Tariffs ↑, domestic manufacturing ↑, deficit ↑, Fed pressure ↑, debasement accelerates → hard assets
- Democratic win (2026 archetype): Green energy ↑, international cooperation ↑, regulatory ↑, deficit less acute → mix
- Wave election (either direction): Maximum policy uncertainty → gold, volatility long

**Election positioning timing:**
- 6 months before: Position on policy platform divergence (low risk, low cost)
- 30 days before: Reduce position size (polling volatility risk)
- 2 weeks before: Hold core, hedge with options
- Post-result: Close hedges, press conviction on winner's policy trades
