## The Asymmetry Score (1–10)

**Definition**: How mispriced is an asset relative to what the GT framework predicts?

**Formula**:
> Asymmetry Score = [(GT Probability) ÷ (Market-Implied Probability)] × (Historical Undervaluation Factor)

**Calculating GT Probability:**
Sum of supporting signals (0–5 each):
- GT structural thesis active and confirmed: +5
- Dominant strategy analysis unambiguous: +4
- Multiple laws converging: +3
- Historical analogue confirms: +2
- Monitoring signals in place: +1
Max raw score: 15 → normalize to 0–100%

**Calculating Market-Implied Probability:**
Proxy signals (use best available):
- Options implied move vs. GT predicted move
- Consensus analyst target vs. GT predicted level
- Current price vs. pre-thesis baseline
- Investor positioning (COT, ETF flows, 13F) vs. GT-predicted positioning
Scale 0–100%

**Calculating Historical Undervaluation Factor:**
- Asset at/above historical fair value relative to GT scenario: 1.0×
- Asset 10–25% below GT-implied fair value: 1.2×
- Asset 25–50% below GT-implied fair value: 1.5×
- Asset >50% below GT-implied fair value (major mispricing): 2.0×

**Score interpretation:**
| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9–10 | Maximum mispricing — GT high conviction, market ignoring | Tier 1 — full position |
| 7–8 | Strong mispricing — clear thesis, market partially priced | Tier 1 — position with adds |
| 5–6 | Moderate mispricing — thesis confirmed but partially priced | Tier 2 — watch for catalyst |
| 3–4 | Mild mispricing — thesis partially confirmed or contested | Tier 2 — starter position only |
| 1–2 | Fully priced in — thesis confirmed but market already moved | Hold existing; no new entry |

**Worked-example format (illustrative only — regenerate from live data):**
A properly scored entry reads like: *"[TICKER]: Score ~X — [current % off ATH], [thesis], [positioning/coverage observation]."* The names and numbers below are deliberately omitted because any stored example goes stale immediately and risks being mistaken for a current call. When you run the skill, produce fresh entries from today's prices, today's positioning, and the current status of the thesis, and stamp each with the retrieval date.

## M×E×C Player Scoring

**How to score each player:**

**Mass (M) — 1–10:**
- 1: Isolated individual actor with no allies
- 3: Small state/group with regional support
- 5: Mid-size power with significant but limited coalition
- 7: Major power with broad alliance network
- 10: Global hegemon with universal financial/military reach

**Energy (E) — 1–10:**
- 1: Existential threat to regime, no popular support for action
- 3: Low motivation; action is optional/opportunistic
- 5: Clear interest in outcome; moderate commitment
- 7: High motivation; significant domestic pressure to act
- 10: Existential stakes; script-locked; will act regardless of cost

**Coordination (C) — 1–10:**
- 1: Complete internal dysfunction; factions actively working against each other
- 3: Poorly coordinated; different agencies/groups pulling in different directions
- 5: Moderate coordination; unified on broad direction but tactical disputes
- 7: Strong coordination; unified command, shared doctrine, clear hierarchy
- 10: Perfect synchronicity; shared narrative + shared command + shared resources

**Weighted Score** = [M + (2×E) + (4×C)] ÷ 7

**Example calculations** (hypothetical, to show the mechanic — not a claim about any live conflict):
- Side A: M=9, E=5, C=5 → [9 + 10 + 20] ÷ 7 = 5.6
- Side B: M=4, E=9, C=7 → [4 + 18 + 28] ÷ 7 = 7.1
→ Reading: the higher-coordination, higher-energy side (B) can outperform raw-mass superiority (A) — the Law-of-Asymmetry pattern. Round to one decimal; the inputs are subjective 1–10 judgments, so further precision is illusory. Treat this as one input among several, and always sanity-check it against the base rate for conflicts of this type.

## Confidence Level Calibration

**High Confidence (>75% probability):**
Requires ALL of:
- At least 3 GT laws actively supporting the prediction
- At least 1 structural thesis fully confirmed
- Historical analogue with >70% hit rate
- Monitoring signals already confirming
- Player dominant strategies are unambiguous (no rational alternative)

**Medium Confidence (50–75%):**
Requires:
- At least 2 GT laws supporting
- At least 1 structural thesis partially confirmed
- Some monitoring signals active
- Player dominant strategies are likely but not certain

**Low Confidence (25–50%):**
When:
- 1 GT law supporting
- Structural thesis is early-stage
- Monitoring signals not yet active
- Player dominant strategies are contested

**Flag for monitoring only (<25%):**
When:
- GT framework only directionally suggestive
- Thesis is structural setup but no active laws engaged
- Build position as signals confirm

## Entry Timing Framework

**Phase 1: Structural Setup (6–12 months before catalyst)**
- GT analysis confirms structural setup score >7
- No immediate catalyst visible
- Action: Small starter position (10–20% of target size)
- Why: Maximum asymmetry; IV lowest; market immune

**Phase 2: Early Confirmation (3–6 months before catalyst)**
- First monitoring signals firing
- Institutional accumulation beginning (COT/ETF flows)
- Action: Add to 40–60% of target size
- Why: Thesis confirming but still well ahead of consensus

**Phase 3: Catalyst Approaching (1–3 months before)**
- Multiple monitoring signals active
- Media beginning to notice
- IV beginning to rise
- Action: Complete to 80–100% of target; consider options structure
- Why: Maximize position before consensus catch-up re-prices

**Phase 4: Catalyst Event**
- Hold through event; do not add at maximum IV
- Event either confirms or challenges thesis
- If confirmed: Hold, let repricing complete
- If challenged: Evaluate falsification conditions before exiting

**Phase 5: Post-Catalyst**
- If thesis intact: Hold Tier 1 positions; trim speculative positions
- If thesis complete: Rotate to next GT-predicted opportunity
- If thesis invalidated: Exit on first monitoring signal miss, not thesis challenge

## The Leading Indicator Calendar System

**The 60-Day Rule (generalized)**: For every GT prediction, identify one specific, observable, dated metric that serves as the primary thesis health signal approximately 60 days after a major catalyst.

**Template**: "60 days after [catalyst], [specific metric] should be [level]. If it's above [threshold A], thesis is intact. If it's below [threshold B], thesis is stalling."

**Reference examples:**
- Iran war / oil thesis: 60d post-ceasefire → retail gas price trajectory (>$4.50 = intact, <$3.50 = stalling)
- Fed trapped thesis: Monthly CPI print (>3.0% for 3 consecutive months = thesis intact; <2.5% = reassess)
- De-dollarization: 30-year Treasury bid-to-cover ratio (declining trend = confirming; rising = challenging)
- Deglobalization: ISM Manufacturing PMI new orders vs. imports subindex divergence (widening = confirming)

**Build a Leading Indicator Calendar for every prediction:**
| Timing | Signal | Confirm Threshold | Challenge Threshold | Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [X] days/weeks | [Specific metric] | [Level] | [Level] | [Where to find it] |

## Falsification Standards

**A valid falsification condition must be:**
1. **Specific**: Not "if the situation improves" but "if WTI crude drops below $75/bbl for 30+ consecutive days"
2. **Observable**: Available from public data sources within 1 week
3. **Binary**: Either it happened or it didn't
4. **Pre-committed**: Written before the prediction, not rationalized after

**Three types of falsification:**
- **Full invalidation**: Exits all positions immediately (e.g., "If the Fed cuts 3× in 6 months → debasement thesis inverts")
- **Thesis weakening**: Reduces exposure by 50% (e.g., "If BRICS summit produces no de-dollarization language → timeline extends significantly")
- **Thesis acceleration**: Adds to positions (e.g., "If the 30yr Treasury auction fails to attract sufficient bids → debasement acceleration confirmed")

**The falsification pre-commitment test**: Before entering a position, write out: "I will exit this trade if [specific condition]. I will NOT rationalize continued holding by arguing [typical excuse]." Pre-committing to falsification conditions prevents narrative-following bias from trapping you in deteriorating positions.
