# MAG8 — AI Video Generation Prompts

Aside-style launch campaign, built on MAG8's real design system (`globals.css` tokens, the
`HeroConfluence` braid, public lens vocabulary only — white-label safe).

- **Long form:** "The Signal" — 21 clips × 8s = **2:48**, 16:9, for YouTube.
- **Short:** 4 clips × 8s = **32s**, 9:16 vertical, for Shorts / Reels / TikTok.
- Gold `#f2c75c` is held back until the fusion moment (S10 / V3) — the brand rule
  ("gold = verdicts only") is the emotional payoff of the film.

---

## How to assemble each generation

1. Every generation = **STYLE HEADER + SCENE PROMPT + NEGATIVE FOOTER** pasted together as
   one text. Keep the header and footer handy and paste the trio every time.
2. **Generate in order.** Where a scene starts on an object (not an empty frame), screenshot
   the previous clip's LAST FRAME and feed it as the start-frame / image reference. Cuts that
   land on empty void or white frames are seamless by construction.
3. **Chapter smash cuts are intentional:** S14→S15 (dark→white) and S17→S18 (white→dark) are
   hard cuts on the beat — no frame matching needed there.
4. **Mute all generated audio.** Lay one continuous music track across the whole edit in
   post; that's what makes 25 clips read as one film. (Per-clip AUDIO lines below are there
   in case your generator produces audio you want to keep as a scratch layer.)
5. **Attach real screenshots** where a scene says `ATTACH:` — they anchor the generator to
   the actual product UI.

```text

locked-off tripod camera (slow push-ins only where stated), smooth eased motion, 4K detail,

explicitly calls for it. All glows are soft additive light with gentle bloom. Keep all
critical action and text inside the central square safe zone.
```
